# Ashvara - brand voice for product videos

Read before writing any row in `plan.csv`. Store: https://ashvara.store (home & lifestyle: Pets, Self-Care,
Home Fitness, plus bundles and gift guides).

## Who is talking
A friendly person showing a friend something useful they found. The words are read by an AI voice over the
store's product photos and stock clips, so every line must sound natural spoken aloud.

## Who we are talking to
US shoppers 25-55 scrolling Reels, TikTok and Shorts: dog and cat owners, people building a self-care routine,
people working out at home, and gift shoppers in Q4.

## How it sounds
- Open with a relatable moment or problem in under 12 words ("Muddy walk. Clean floors. Ten seconds.").
- Then show the product working: one concrete detail per line (size, material, how it's used).
- End with the price, said the way people say it ("twenty-seven ninety-nine"), and "Link in bio, ashvara dot store."
- 20-35 seconds (about 55-90 spoken words). Contractions always. No emojis or hashtags in narration.

## What we never do
- No medical, health, therapy or "clinically proven" claims. No promised results ("cures", "fixes pain",
  "reverses aging", "melts fat").
- No invented reviews, star ratings, sales numbers or fake urgency ("only 3 left", "ends tonight").
- No other brands, characters or celebrities. No kids' toys.
- Prices must match the store. The bot adds the live price and product link automatically.

## Row format (plan.csv)
format = `product`; `product` = the Shopify handle (the part after /products/ in the URL); `script` = one line
per scene; `footage` = 2-3 short stock searches for the non-product scenes (e.g. "dog sleeping bed").
